Pick the apps. Pick the habit. Take the photo.

No honor system: do the habit, then show it to the camera.

  1. Step 01

    Pick the apps and the habit

    Toll locks the apps you pick with Apple's Screen Time; everything else stays open. Then pick the habit, from the gym to a walk with the dog.

  2. Step 02

    Miss it, and they lock

    The block screen has two buttons: Close, and Open Toll to prove your habit. No snooze, no "five more minutes", no limit you can tap past.

  3. Step 03

    Take the photo

    With Toll's camera, by midnight. Your iPhone checks it and Toll never keeps it. The gym takes a check-in and a check-out, at least 30 minutes apart.

  4. Step 04

    Your apps stay unlocked

    Once today's habit is proved, they stay unlocked for the rest of the day and your streak grows by one. Schedules, daily limits and sessions you set still apply.

Miss a day, and the next one starts locked.

Until you prove the habit. While your apps are locked you can't talk your way out: making a habit easier waits until midnight, and with Strict mode on, so does removing apps. Or lock them every morning until that day's proof.

  • 2 short breaks a day

Private by design. Nothing to sign up for.

Toll has no account and no server of its own. What it keeps stays with you.

  • Photos never leave your iPhone

    Each photo is checked on your iPhone, then discarded. Never stored, never uploaded.

Questions. Plain answers.

Still have a question? Write to us, or read the support page.

Doesn't my iPhone already do this?

Screen Time's own limits come with Ignore Limit: one more minute, or the rest of the day. Toll uses Screen Time to lock the apps, but its block screen has no such button: Close, or Open Toll to take the photo.

How does Toll check the photo?

With Toll's own camera, live only: there is no photo library, so an old photo can't count. Your iPhone checks that the photo shows the habit, with Apple's on-device model on iPhones with Apple Intelligence and Apple's on-device image analysis on others. Then the photo is discarded.

The gym takes a check-in and a check-out at least 30 minutes apart, meals one photo per meal, study one photo of your desk with a laptop, book or notes.

When do my apps lock?

After a missed day: take each day's photos by midnight and the next day starts unlocked; miss one, and the next day starts locked until that day's habits are done. A habit never locks anything on the day it was made. To lock every morning until that day's photo instead: Settings, When apps lock, Every morning.

Schedules lock between their times, time limits once their minutes are used, and sessions until they end.

Can I take a day off?

Two rest days a month. A rest day covers one day: nothing is due and the streak keeps. For a few minutes instead, take a short break: up to two a day, 5 to 15 minutes each, at least an hour apart (not while Strict mode holds your apps).

What happens to my photos and data?

Photos are checked on your iPhone and never stored or uploaded. Your habits and records stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Toll has no account, no ads and no trackers; it sends anonymous usage counts, never who you are. The privacy policy lists every count.

What happens if I delete Toll?

Every lock goes with it. With Strict mode on, iOS lets you delete apps only while they're unlocked. With iCloud on, your habits, rules and photo records come back when you install Toll again; you pick the apps to lock again.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toll. During the yearly plan's free week, Toll reminds you 2 days before the first charge. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

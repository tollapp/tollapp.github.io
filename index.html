Toll for iPhone

Gate opens at proof.

Toll keeps the apps you choose behind a gate until you prove today's habit with a photo from the live camera, checked on your iPhone.

  • Pick the apps and the habits

    The gym, meals, study, a run, a walk with the dog, or a habit of your own. While one is due, the apps you chose are shielded.

  • Prove it with a photo

    From the live camera, checked on the iPhone by Apple's on-device models. Photos are never stored and never leave the phone.

  • The gate opens for the day

    Streaks, ranks and a daily score for the days you keep. Focus sessions shut the gate on demand; two rest days a month keep the streak.

  • Strict by choice, never a trap

    Gate lock and Hard lock are off until you turn them on, and each has a way out that needs no one. Deleting Toll removes every shield.

  • No account, no tracking

    No sign-up, no server of its own, no ads, no analytics. Your history stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

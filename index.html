No ignore button. Prove it instead.

The usual screen time limit is one tap away from "ignore for today". Toll's block screen has two buttons: Close, and Open Toll to prove your habit.

  • No snooze, no "five more minutes", no limit you can tap past.
  • Prove today's habit and your apps unlock for the rest of the day. Schedules, daily limits and focus sessions you set still apply.
  • Live camera only. Checked on your iPhone, never stored or uploaded.

Schedules and limits

Lock apps on a schedule, or once they've had their minutes for the day. No photo needed; they unlock by themselves.

Focus sessions

Lock your apps for 15 minutes to 3 hours, photo or not. They unlock by themselves; ending early takes a deliberate hold.

Toll Score

Out of 100: the share of your habits kept over the last seven days. Rest days and paused habits don't count.

The block screen

What you see when you open a locked app: why it's locked, when it unlocks, and a line you choose: your own reason, a short poem, the Gospels, the Quran and more.

PIN lock

A PIN only before something that loosens your rules. Never to prove a habit, and a forgotten PIN lifts after 24 hours.

Off until you turn it on.

Strict mode

While your apps are locked, iOS blocks deleting Toll and your apps, keeps the date and time automatic, and sessions run to the end. It ends the moment your apps unlock.

Off until you turn it on.

Website protection

Adult websites filtered all the time, and every website blocked while your apps are locked. Toll never learns which sites you visit.

Set it up once. Prove it every day.

No honor system. Do the habit, then show it to the camera.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Choose the apps and the habit

    Toll shields the apps that take your hours with Apple's Screen Time. Then pick the habit that opens the gate, from the gym to a walk with the dog.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Prove it with a photo

    From the live camera, by midnight. Toll checks it on your iPhone and never keeps it. The gym takes a check-in and a check-out.

  3. 3

    Step 3: The gate opens

    Your apps are back for the day, the streak grows by one, and tomorrow starts open.

Miss a day, and the next one starts shut.

While the gate is shut, it can't be talked down. Removing apps or making a habit easier waits until midnight, and loosening a schedule or a limit waits until it lets go.

With Toll Pro, the gate can shut every morning instead, until that day's proof.

Focus sessions

Shut the gate for 15 minutes to 3 hours, proof or not. It opens by itself; opening early takes a deliberate hold.

Toll Score

How the day went, out of 100: proof, sessions and the last seven days. Under it, the week's proofs and sessions.

The gate screen

Why the gate is shut, when it opens, and a line you choose: your own reason, a short poem, or with Toll Pro the Gospels, the Quran and more.

Gate lock

Toll Pro

A PIN only before something that loosens the gate. Never to prove a habit, and a forgotten PIN lifts after 24 hours.

The sealed gate

Toll Pro

While the gate is shut, iOS blocks deleting Toll and your apps, keeps the date and time automatic, and sessions run to the end. The seal lifts the moment the gate opens.

Website protection

Toll Pro

Adult websites filtered all the time, and every website shut while the gate is shut. Toll never learns which sites you visit.

Private by design.

Toll has no account and no server of its own. What it keeps stays with you.

Photos stay on your iPhone

Each proof is checked on your iPhone, then discarded. Never stored, never uploaded.

No account, no login

Your habits and records live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, so they follow you to a new phone.

No ads, no trackers

No analytics either. Toll does not track you and does not sell or share anything.

What Toll never does

  • Hides your apps. It shields them; they stay on your Home Screen.
  • Asks for the PIN to prove a habit or to open Toll.
  • Sends a notification you did not ask for.
  • Keeps a shield once Toll is deleted.

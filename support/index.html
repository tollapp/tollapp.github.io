Locked apps

How does Toll lock apps?

Pick the apps to lock and the rules that lock them: habits you prove with a photo, schedules and time limits. While a rule holds, those apps are locked. Take the habit's photo and they unlock, unless a schedule or a limit still holds them.

When do my apps lock?

A schedule locks them between its times, a time limit once its minutes are used, a session until it ends. Habits lock them after a missed day: take each day's photos by midnight and the next day starts unlocked; miss one, and the next day starts locked until that day's habits are done, or, on a day with none due, until one photo of the habit you missed. A habit never locks anything on the day it was made.

To lock every morning until that day's photo instead: Settings, When apps lock, Every morning.

What are schedules and time limits?

A schedule locks your apps between two times on the days you pick, like a workday or the night. A time limit locks them for the rest of the day once they've been used for its minutes. Neither asks for a photo; both unlock by themselves.

Why can't I remove an app right now?

With Strict mode on or in a Strict session, removing apps while they are locked waits until midnight; with a PIN lock, removing apps asks for the PIN first. While your apps are locked, making a habit easier waits until midnight, and making a schedule or a limit that holds them easier waits until it ends, so you can't undo your rules in the moment. Adding apps works at once.

What does the block screen say?

Why the app is locked and when it unlocks, and a line from the words you pick in Settings, Block screen: Your reason, Short poems, Did you know, The Gospels, The Quran, Old wisdom, Something else (a small thing to do instead), Night sky, Groaners or Classic novels. Open Toll takes you back to take the photo.

What do PIN lock and Strict mode do?

Both are off unless you turn them on. PIN lock asks for its PIN only before a change that makes your rules easier, never to prove a habit or to open Toll; a forgotten PIN lifts after 24 hours.

Strict mode stops Toll and every app from being deleted, keeps the date and time automatic and keeps sessions locked to the end while your apps are locked.

Day to day

What counts as proof?

A photo from Toll's camera, checked on your iPhone. By default the gym takes a check-in and a check-out at least 30 minutes apart, meals one photo per meal, study one photo of your desk with a laptop, book or notes. The other kinds, from a run to a glass of water, and a habit of your own take a photo that shows it done. Photos are never stored.

A habit can also count without a photo: by your stay at a place you pick, or by your steps from Apple Health. See the next answer.

Can I prove a habit without a photo?

Yes, if you want to. Edit the habit, then under Proof without a photo, pick At a place or With steps. Both are optional, and the photo always works too.

At a place: search for the place, like your gym or a library, or use where you are now. Toll checks you in when you arrive, and the habit is done once you've stayed 15 minutes to 2 hours, as you choose. iPhone then asks for your location: pick Allow While Using App, then Change to Always Allow, so it works with Toll closed.

With steps, for a walk or a run: pick a goal from 3,000 to 15,000 steps a day and allow Apple Health. The habit is done once Health counts your goal. Steps typed into Health by hand don't count.

A new place or goal, or an easier one, saved while the habit is due, counts from midnight.

Can I take a day off?

Two rest days a month, under Rest days. A rest day covers one day: today's counts from the moment you take it, tomorrow's from midnight. Nothing is due that day and the streak keeps.

For a few minutes instead, take a short break: up to two a day, 5 to 15 minutes each, at least an hour apart (not while Strict mode holds your apps).

What is a focus session?

A session locks your apps for a set time, 15 minutes to 3 hours, no photo needed. Start one from Sessions. They unlock by themselves at the end; to unlock early, you hold the button down.

Which notifications does Toll send?

Only the ones switched on in Settings, Notifications. There are nine, each with its own switch: Evening reminder, on days a habit is still due; Due soon, an hour before midnight if a habit is still due; Streak at risk, before a streak ends; Morning plan, today's habits each morning; Weekly recap, your week on Sunday evening; Rules, before a schedule locks your apps and when a time limit is reached; Session over, when a focus session ends; At a place, when you've stayed long enough at a habit's place; and Trial reminder, 2 days before the yearly plan's free week ends.

Streak at risk, Weekly recap and At a place are on once you allow notifications, and Trial reminder when you ask for it. The others are off until you turn them on.

Setting up

What does Toll need?

An iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later and Screen Time access for Toll, which iOS asks for once: tap Continue, then Face ID or your passcode. It's your own Screen Time, not parental control. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the photo check uses Apple's on-device model; on other iPhones it uses Apple's on-device image analysis.

Location and Apple Health, only if you give a habit a place or a step goal.

Which languages?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese and Korean. Toll follows your iPhone's language.

An app isn't locked

In Toll, open Settings and tap Lock again. If it says it couldn't, open the Settings app, then Screen Time, then Apps with Screen Time Access, and make sure Toll is on.

What happens if I delete Toll?

Every lock goes with it. With Strict mode on, iOS lets you delete apps only while they're unlocked. With iCloud on, your habits, rules and photo records come back when you install Toll again; you pick the apps to lock again.

Toll Pro

What does Toll Pro include?

All of Toll: every habit, your own included; proof at a place or with steps; schedules and time limits; Strict mode, locking every morning and the PIN lock; website protection; focus sessions, rest days, short breaks and the score; and every block screen, the Gospels and the Quran included. Toll needs it after the first four screens; the yearly plan starts with 7 days free.

What does it cost?

Yearly: $29.99 a year, after 7 days free. Monthly: $5.99 a month. Lifetime: $79.99 once, no subscription. These are US prices; the App Store shows yours. One free week per Apple Account, on the yearly plan.

I started on the free plan. Do I keep it?

Yes. If you finished setting up Toll before version 1.2, the free plan stays with your iPhone as it was: one habit of Toll's own kinds, locked apps until its photo, focus sessions, rest days, the score and four sets of words for the block screen. Toll Pro adds the rest whenever you like. Deleting Toll ends it.

What happens when Toll Pro ends?

Your Pro rules pause: their apps unlock, and Strict mode, the PIN and website protection stop. Your habits, rules, photo records and settings are kept and work again the moment Toll Pro is back. Nothing is deleted. On the free plan from before version 1.2, one habit keeps working (the oldest of Toll's own kinds) and the rest wait, paused, until Toll Pro is back.

If your iPhone is offline when Toll Pro ends, your Pro rules can keep working for up to 3 days, until Toll can check.

How do I cancel Toll Pro?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toll. The free week comes with the yearly plan only; during it, Toll reminds you 2 days before the first charge. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

In Toll, open Settings and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought Toll Pro. There is no Toll account to sign in to.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

No account, no server of its own, photos checked on your iPhone and never stored. The details are in the privacy policy.

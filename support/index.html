Using Toll

How does the gate work?

Pick the apps to put behind the gate and the habits that open it. While a habit is due, those apps are shielded. Prove it with a photo and the gate opens for the rest of the day.

What counts as proof?

A photo from the live camera, checked on the iPhone. The gym takes a check-in and a check-out at least 30 minutes apart, meals one photo per meal, study one photo of your desk with a laptop, book or notes. The other kinds, from a run to a glass of water, and your own habit take a photo that shows it done. Photos are never stored.

An app is not shielded

In Toll, open Settings and tap Re-apply the gate. If it says Could not apply, open the Settings app, then Screen Time, then Apps with Screen Time Access, and make sure Toll is on.

Can I take a day off?

Two rest days a month, from the rest day pass. Today starts in 30 minutes, tomorrow at midnight. Nothing is due that day and the streak keeps.

What is a focus session?

The Timer shuts the gate for a set time, 15 minutes to 3 hours, proof or not. It opens by itself at the end; opening early takes a deliberate hold.

Why can I not remove an app now?

While a habit is due, removing apps or making a habit easier waits until midnight, so the gate cannot be talked down in the moment. Adding apps works at once.

What do the locks do?

Both are off unless you turn them on. Gate lock asks for a PIN before anything that loosens the gate; a forgotten PIN lifts after 24 hours. Hard lock stops apps from being deleted and keeps the date and time automatic while the gate is shut.

What happens if I delete Toll?

Every shield goes with it. With Hard lock on, iOS lets you delete apps only while the gate is open. With iCloud on, your habits and proof records come back when you install Toll again; the apps behind the gate are picked again.

What does Toll need?

An iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later and Screen Time access for Toll, which iOS asks for once. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the photo check uses Apple's on-device model; on other iPhones it uses Apple's on-device image analysis.

Toll Pro

How do I cancel Toll Pro?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toll. During the free week, a reminder comes before the first charge. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

In Toll, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought Toll Pro. There is no Toll account to sign in to.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

No account, no server of its own, photos checked on your iPhone and never stored. The details are in the privacy policy.

The gate

How does the gate work?

Pick the apps to put behind the gate and the rules that shut it: habits you prove with a photo, schedules and time limits. While the gate is shut, those apps are shielded. Prove the habit and the gate opens, unless a schedule or a limit still holds it.

When does the gate shut?

A schedule shuts it between its times, a time limit once its minutes are used, a session until it ends. Habits shut it after a missed day: prove each day's habits by midnight and the next day starts open; miss one, and the next day starts shut until that day's habits are proved, or, on a day with none due, until one proof of the one you missed. A habit never shuts it because of the day it was made. With Toll Pro it can shut every morning until that day's proof instead: choose Every morning under Settings, When the gate shuts.

What are schedules and time limits?

A schedule shuts the gate between two times on the days you pick, like a workday or the night. A time limit shuts it for the rest of the day once the apps behind the gate were used for its minutes. Neither asks for a photo; both open by themselves.

Why can I not remove an app now?

While the gate is shut, removing apps or making a habit easier waits until midnight, and a change to a schedule or a limit that holds the gate waits until it lets go, so the gate cannot be talked down in the moment. Adding apps works at once.

What does the gate screen say?

Why the gate is shut and when it opens, and a line from the words you pick in Settings, Gate screen: your own reason, short poems or did you know, and with Toll Pro the Gospels, the Quran, old wisdom, something else to do, the night sky, groaners or classic novels. Open Toll brings you back to prove it.

What do the locks do?

Both are part of Toll Pro and off unless you turn them on. Gate lock asks for its PIN only before something that loosens the gate, never to prove a habit or to open Toll; a forgotten PIN lifts after 24 hours. The sealed gate stops Toll and every app from being deleted, keeps the date and time automatic and keeps sessions shut to the end while the gate is shut.

Day to day

What counts as proof?

A photo from the live camera, checked on the iPhone. The gym takes a check-in and a check-out at least 30 minutes apart, meals one photo per meal, study one photo of your desk with a laptop, book or notes. The other kinds, from a run to a glass of water, and with Toll Pro your own habit take a photo that shows it done. Photos are never stored.

Can I take a day off?

Two rest day tokens a month, under Rest days in Apps or Settings. A token covers one day: today's counts from the moment you spend it, tomorrow's from midnight. Nothing is due that day and the streak keeps.

What is a focus session?

A session shuts the gate for a set time, 15 minutes to 3 hours, proof or not. Start one from Sessions. It opens by itself at the end; opening early takes a deliberate hold.

Which notifications does Toll send?

Only the ones switched on in Settings, Notifications: the evening reminder on days a habit is still due, a note before a schedule and when a limit is reached, the end of a session, and a reminder before the yearly plan's free week ends.

Setting up

What does Toll need?

An iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later and Screen Time access for Toll, which iOS asks for once. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the photo check uses Apple's on-device model; on other iPhones it uses Apple's on-device image analysis.

An app is not shielded

In Toll, open Settings and tap Re-apply the gate. If it says Could not apply, open the Settings app, then Screen Time, then Apps with Screen Time Access, and make sure Toll is on.

What happens if I delete Toll?

Every shield goes with it. With the gate sealed, iOS lets you delete apps only while the gate is open. With iCloud on, your habits, rules and proof records come back when you install Toll again; the apps behind the gate are picked again.

Toll Pro

What does Toll Pro add?

More habits, your own included; schedules and time limits; the sealed gate, the strict gate that shuts every morning and the PIN lock; website protection; and every gate screen, the Gospels and the Quran included. The free plan keeps one habit and everything else: the gate, proof by photo, focus sessions, rest days, Toll Score and four sets of words for the gate screen.

What happens when Toll Pro ends?

One habit keeps working: the oldest of Toll's own kinds. Every other habit, schedule and time limit is paused: kept as it is, never due, and back the moment Toll Pro is. The sealed gate, the PIN and website protection turn off, and every morning gives way to after a missed day. Nothing is deleted.

How do I cancel Toll Pro?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Toll. The free week comes with the yearly plan only; during it, a reminder comes before the first charge. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore a purchase?

In Toll, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought Toll Pro. There is no Toll account to sign in to.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
